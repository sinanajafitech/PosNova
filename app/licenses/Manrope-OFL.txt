Manrope font family, bundled under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope (Manrope[wght].ttf)
Static weight instances (manrope_light/regular/medium/semibold/bold/extrabold.ttf)
were generated locally from the upstream variable font using fonttools'
varLib.instancer, matching the weights used by the POSNova design system's type
scale (Regular 400, Medium 500, SemiBold 600, plus Light/Bold/ExtraBold for
completeness). Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org
